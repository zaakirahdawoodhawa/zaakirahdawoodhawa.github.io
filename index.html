The story so far

Four chapters,
one throughline.

01 Cape Town · 2019 — 2021

The foundation

Portfolio Coordinator — SAPIN

I started where every rand is accounted for: property. End-to-end projects — renovations, tenant campaigns, contractor negotiations, risk frameworks and financial models. When you’re responsible for the whole outcome, you learn fast that details are never small.

Ownership means the outcome — not the task list.

02 Cape Town · 2022 — 2023

The craft

Communications & CX Manager — Sanlam

At one of Africa’s largest financial services groups, I ran campaigns end to end — copy, design, A/B testing, compliance — and produced corporate events from budget to final applause. My favourite build: a knowledge hub that became the onboarding backbone for client-facing teams.

Consistency is kindness — to your audience and your team.

03 Calgary · 2024

The leap

Advanced AI Data Trainer & Quality Analyst — Invisible Technologies

Then I moved continents — and went straight to the technology reshaping my field. I trained and evaluated AI models: designing prompts, stress-testing outputs, building feedback loops and QA protocols. I didn’t just adopt the new stack. I helped teach it.

AI rewards what great marketing rewards: clarity, structure, taste.

04 Calgary · 2025 — 2026

The build

Marketing Lead — SES Inc. (Strategic Estimating Software)

A cost-estimating software firm with 40 years of expertise, ready to look like its future. I built the brand system from the ground up — identity, guidelines, brochure, website — then took QPlant to market with launch decks, film and events. From blank page to full brand, shipped.

Small teams don’t need more hands. They need owners.

How I work

Principles I don’t
compromise on.

1

Own it, end to end

From ideation to execution to the post-mortem. If my name is on it, the outcome is mine — including the parts nobody asked me to fix.

2

Simplify, then systemize

Chaos is a choice. I turn fragmented workflows into intake forms, knowledge hubs and dashboards — so good work becomes repeatable.

3

Obsess over people

Behind every metric is a human. I write for readers, design for users, and plan events for the person in the back row.

4

Raise the bar

Perfectionist, recovering — but only slightly. “Good enough” ships when it’s earned, not when it’s convenient.
